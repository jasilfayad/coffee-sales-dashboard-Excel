# Coffee Sales Dashboard — Excel

An interactive coffee sales dashboard built using Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance across coffee types, roast types, sizes, countries, customers, and years.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview
![Coffee Sales Dashboard](./dashboard.png)
## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project was to transform coffee sales data into an interactive Excel dashboard that provides a clear overview of sales performance and helps identify trends across different coffee products, countries, and time periods.

## 💡 Key Insights

### 1. Overall Sales Performance

The dashboard records total coffee sales of approximately **$45,134** across the period from **2019 to 2022**. The analysis covers multiple coffee types, countries, customers, roast types, and product sizes, providing an overall view of sales performance.

The results show that sales are heavily concentrated in a small number of markets, while product performance varies considerably from year to year.

### 2. United States Is the Dominant Market

The **United States is the strongest-performing country**, generating approximately **$35,639** in sales. This represents roughly **79% of the total sales** displayed in the dashboard.

Ireland generated approximately **$6,697**, while the United Kingdom generated approximately **$2,799**.

This shows a substantial difference in sales contribution between the United States and the other countries. The U.S. market is therefore the most important market in terms of revenue contribution and represents the largest opportunity for maintaining and expanding sales.

### 3. Sales Performance Changed Significantly Over Time

The sales trend shows considerable variation between **2019 and 2022** across the four coffee types: **Arabica, Excelsa, Liberica, and Robusta**.

Rather than following a consistent upward trend, the coffee types experienced increases and decreases at different points during the period. The most noticeable pattern is the strong performance around **2021**, followed by a significant decline in **2022**.

This indicates that sales performance was not stable throughout the period and that understanding the reasons behind the 2022 decline could be important for future business planning.

### 4. Arabica Experienced Strong Growth Before a Major Decline

Arabica sales increased from approximately **$2,900 in 2019** to more than **$4,000 in 2021**, making 2021 its strongest year in the dashboard.

However, sales dropped sharply in 2022 to approximately **$1,450**.

This represents a significant change in performance. The strong growth between 2019 and 2021 suggests increasing demand during that period, while the sharp decline in 2022 indicates that further investigation may be required to understand the factors affecting Arabica sales.

### 5. Liberica Showed Strong Recovery in 2021

Liberica followed a different pattern from Arabica. Sales declined from approximately **$3,400 in 2019 to $2,600 in 2020**, before recovering strongly to approximately **$3,800 in 2021**.

Although Liberica also experienced a decline in 2022, its sales remained around **$2,250**, which was higher than the other coffee types shown for that year.

This makes Liberica an interesting product category because it demonstrated the ability to recover strongly after a decline.

### 6. Excelsa Had Strong Early Performance but Declined After 2020

Excelsa generated approximately **$3,500 in 2019** and increased to around **$3,700 in 2020**.

After reaching this high level, sales declined in 2021 and fell further in 2022 to approximately **$1,900**.

The pattern suggests that Excelsa performed well during the earlier years but struggled to maintain that performance in the later period.

### 7. Robusta Had Relatively Stable Sales Before Declining

Robusta showed a comparatively stable sales pattern between **2019 and 2021**, remaining around the **$2,400–$2,500** range.

However, sales declined to approximately **$1,700 in 2022**.

Compared with the other coffee types, Robusta did not experience the same sharp increase during 2021, but it also maintained relatively consistent performance before the overall decline in 2022.

### 8. 2021 Was a Strong Year for Several Coffee Types

The dashboard indicates that **2021 was an important year for coffee sales performance**.

Arabica reached its highest point at more than **$4,000**, while Liberica also reached approximately **$3,800**. Excelsa and Robusta remained at comparatively strong levels during the same period.

The performance across multiple coffee types suggests that 2021 was a particularly strong period before the broad decline seen in 2022.

### 9. 2022 Shows a Broad Decline Across All Coffee Types

One of the clearest patterns in the dashboard is the decline in sales during **2022**.

Arabica, Excelsa, Liberica, and Robusta all recorded lower sales in 2022 compared with their stronger performance in previous years.

Because the decline affects all four coffee types, it may indicate that the change was not limited to a single product. Further analysis could therefore investigate whether factors such as country, roast type, product size, or customer purchasing behavior contributed to this decline.

### 10. Liberica Was the Strongest Performer in 2022

Although Liberica's sales declined in 2022 compared with 2021, it still recorded approximately **$2,250**, making it the highest-performing coffee type among the four shown for that year.

Excelsa followed at approximately **$1,900**, while Robusta and Arabica were lower.

This suggests that Liberica maintained comparatively stronger demand during a year when overall product sales were declining.

### 11. Sales Are Concentrated Among a Small Number of Countries

The country-level analysis shows that the **United States contributes the majority of sales**, while Ireland and the United Kingdom contribute considerably less.

This concentration means that the overall business performance is strongly influenced by the U.S. market. At the same time, the lower sales from Ireland and the United Kingdom could represent opportunities for increasing market penetration in those regions.

### 12. Top Customers Have Relatively Similar Sales

Among the top five customers shown in the dashboard, **Allis Wilmore** generated the highest sales at approximately **$317**, followed by **Brenn Dundredge at approximately $307**.

Terri Farra generated approximately **$289**, while **Nelson Cuttler generated around $282** and **Don Flintiff around $278**.

The relatively small difference between these customers indicates that sales among the top five customers are fairly evenly distributed rather than being dominated by a single customer.

### 13. Interactive Filters Support Deeper Analysis

The dashboard includes filters for **Roast Type, Year, and Size**.

These filters allow users to analyze sales from different perspectives. For example, users can select a specific year to investigate yearly performance, choose a roast type to compare product preferences, or select a particular size to understand how product size affects sales.

This makes the dashboard useful not only for viewing overall performance but also for exploring specific segments of the business.

### 14. Overall Business Findings

Overall, the dashboard shows that the coffee business has a strong dependence on the **United States market**, while sales performance varies considerably across coffee types and years.

The strongest period visible in the analysis is **2021**, when several coffee types reached relatively high sales levels. However, the broad decline across all coffee types in **2022** is an important trend that requires further investigation.

From a business perspective, maintaining the strong U.S. market while exploring growth opportunities in Ireland and the United Kingdom could help diversify sales. At the product level, understanding why products such as Arabica and Liberica performed strongly in certain years could also help identify opportunities for improving future sales.
